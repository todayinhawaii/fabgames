🧑‍🍳 How to Play

  1. Study both scenes above — they look identical, but 12 sneaky differences are hiding between them.
  2. Press the big ▶ PLAY button to start the clock.
  3. Click directly on a spot in either scene where something looks different — added, missing, or changed.
  4. A correct click gets circled and celebrated with a sound + sparkles ✨. A wrong click gets a small ✕ and a short cooldown, so don't just click wildly!
